# Rewards And Fulfillment Checklist

Use this checklist before resubmitting the Kickstarter page.

## Reward Structure

Recommended reward ladder:

| Tier | Price | Type | Notes |
| --- | ---: | --- | --- |
| Signal Spark | $5 | Digital | Low-friction support tier with updates and supporter wall |
| Digital Supporter Pack | $15 | Digital | Under-$25 digital tier with wallpapers and thanks |
| Act 1 Digital Key | $25 | Digital | Main game tier |
| Sound And Art Pack | $40 | Digital | Adds soundtrack and mini artbook |
| Beta Fighter | $60 | Digital | Adds beta access and feedback form |
| Marseille Poster Pack | $90 | Physical, EU only | Adds A3 poster |
| Beacon Of Hope Bracelet | $130 | Physical, EU only | Adds bracelet and poster |
| Design Council | $250 | Digital/experience | Feedback session and credit listing |
| Custom NPC + Short Questline | $600 | Digital/experience | Limited quantity, tightly scoped |

## Shipping Rules

- Physical rewards ship to EU countries only at launch.
- Ship-from region: France/EU.
- Poster tier shipping estimate: EUR 8-15.
- Bracelet/poster tier shipping estimate: EUR 10-18.
- Add final country-by-country shipping values before launch if Kickstarter requires exact pricing.
- Use Kickstarter surveys or Pledge Manager for addresses and final reward preferences.
- State that physical rewards are expected after the Act 1 digital release.

## Custom NPC Scope

Use this exact scope on the reward tier:

- One NPC concept inspired by the backer's idea.
- One short side quest or encounter beat.
- Up to 12 short dialogue lines total.
- Up to two revision rounds.
- Team keeps final approval.
- No licensed characters, hate content, explicit sexual content, real-world political targeting, or content that breaks the game's tone.
- If an idea cannot be used, the backer may submit one replacement concept within the same scope.

## Pledge Manager Setup

Prepare these fields before launching Pledge Manager:

- Reward item categories.
- Market value for poster and bracelet.
- EU shipping origins and destination countries.
- Backer survey questions for poster/bracelet preferences.
- Digital file/code delivery method.
- Address lock date before physical production.

## Review Questions For Preview Readers

Ask 3-5 trusted readers to answer these after reading the page:

- What is Divergency?
- What do I receive at the tier I would back?
- When do I receive it?
- What is already built?
- What is still risky?
- Are any rewards unclear or too broad?
- Did any image look like debug footage or unfinished accident rather than intentional work-in-progress?

## Do Not Launch Until

- No bracketed placeholders remain.
- No debug screenshots remain on the public page.
- Every reward has an estimated delivery date.
- EU-only shipping is clearly stated on physical tiers.
- Budget and timeline are visible.
- Risks & Challenges are complete.
- The Custom NPC tier has scope limits and approval rules.
