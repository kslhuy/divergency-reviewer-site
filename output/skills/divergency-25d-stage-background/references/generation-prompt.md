# Reusable generation prompt

Adapt this template to the user's scene. Fill the setting and reference roles from the actual inputs; omit motifs that do not fit. Reference numbering must match the images passed to the tool.

```text
Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: a finished opaque pixel-art environment background for a 2.5D side-scrolling beat 'em up game. Wide 2:1 horizontal composition.

REFERENCE ROLES:
INSPIRATION IMAGE, if supplied: borrow its setting, characteristic architecture, materials, and environmental forms. Recompose them into ONE continuous gameplay room.
STYLE ANCHOR: the blood-lair draft is the primary pixel-art and palette reference. Match its visible pixel scale, hard stair-step contours, compact hand-placed pixel clusters, restrained dithering, discrete shade ramps, dark values, and muted color range. Do not copy brighter or painterly rendering from the inspiration image.
APPROVED GAMEPLAY LAYOUT: the approved catacomb image establishes the side-on viewpoint, depth of the visible combat floor, and placement of large scenery behind it. Do not copy its dragon-bone props into unrelated settings.

SCENE:
Describe the user's requested location here. Preserve the inspiration's distinctive environmental identity. Choose a few large readable silhouettes appropriate to that setting, with smaller environmental details kept subordinate.

GAMEPLAY COMPOSITION:
Fixed side-on beat 'em up view, with a shallow visible ground plane. 2.5D means 2D pixel artwork with room for characters to move left/right and toward/away from the rear wall. The lower 40-50% is a broad, flat, continuous battle floor spanning edge to edge. Texture this floor with materials appropriate to the setting, but keep movement space largely clear. Small flat debris and sparse cracks or low roots only. Place tall props, pillars, giant bones, ruins, water, and hazards behind the rear edge of the floor. Background architecture recedes through gaps behind the midground. Keep foreground framing at the top or extreme corners. No central perspective road, dramatic vanishing point, stacked playable platforms, or pits in the combat floor.

PIXEL STYLE:
Authentic handcrafted 16-bit pixel art matching the STYLE ANCHOR. Visibly square pixel clusters on a consistent grid, crisp hard edges, stepped silhouettes, simple discrete shadow planes, clean limited dithering, and compact highlights. Visual density comparable to a roughly 900 x 450 pixel game background enlarged with nearest-neighbor sampling. Large readable forms and quiet areas on the floor. No fine painterly microtexture, smooth gradients, airbrushing, blur, anti-aliased contours, or 3D rendering.

COLOR AND MOOD:
Match the STYLE ANCHOR's restricted muted palette: near-black charcoal, dark desaturated blue-violet slate, earthy bone gray, muted ochre/brown, dried-blood burgundy, and restrained ghostly teal. Keep hues relevant to the requested setting within this color range. Gray-ochre weathered bones rather than white; muted teal accents rather than electric cyan. Gloomy values with enough separation to read the floor and silhouettes. Discrete pixel lighting, no neon palette or heavy bloom.

DELIVERY:
One finished edge-to-edge opaque environment image. No characters, enemies, ghosts, UI, text, labels, logos, watermark, collage, or map diagram. The result should read as an empty beat 'em up stage ready for character sprites.
```
