#Visual Clarity Checklist

  • Patch set means exactly three embroidered patches, with design choice and repeats allowed; no keychains or stickers.
  • Figure means one selected character, painted resin, 90 mm including base; label all current imagery as concept only.
  • Show A3 dimensions, adjustable cap fit after sampling, and exact included quantities.
  • Keep “already included / additional copy” wording beside add-ons.
  • Use EUR consistently. Remove old USD tier labels from public reward visuals.
  • Digital artbook and lore dossier are PDFs, not printed merchandise.
