#Review Questions For Preview Readers

Ask 3-5 trusted readers to answer these after reading the page:

  • What is Divergency?
  • What do I receive at the tier I would back?
  • Which rewards are digital, physical, or creative participation?
  • Which mockups are guaranteed rewards and which are only concepts or add-ons?
  • When do I receive each reward?
  • What is already built?
  • What is still risky?
  • Are any rewards unclear, too broad, or visually confusing?
  • Did any image look like debug footage or an unfinished accident rather than intentional work-in-progress?
