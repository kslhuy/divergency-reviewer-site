#Current Development Status

Divergency is in active development from a playable build.

Already built or in progress:

  • Five core playable characters, with Ghost planned as a special story fighter.
  • Core 2D/2.5D movement and beat 'em up combat.
  • Skill loadout structure and mission selection UI direction.
  • Player-to-AI squad command prototype.
  • Enemy behavior, encounter logic, and boss encounter tests.
  • Marseille sewer, undercity, laboratory, and industrial environment art.
  • Stage 1 story structure covering Bastonne, Armorlite, Laundel, the port ambush, and the research submarine.
  • Full campaign outline for Sakuri, Calvaria, Akam Meskul, and The Cradle.
  • Combat HUD, command controls, skill selection, and character selection UI direction.

Still to finish:

  • Combat polish, hit timing, balancing, and boss tuning.
  • Final UI art, menu flow, and release-ready presentation.
  • Full stage implementation across the campaign.
  • Final music, sound effects, VFX polish, localization pass, and QA.
  • Backer reward production and delivery.
  • Stretch-goal features, only if those goals are reached and testing supports them.
