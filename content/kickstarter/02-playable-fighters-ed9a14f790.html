#Playable Fighters

#Deep — The War-Tired Gladiator

"I've fought in enough wars to know symbols don't bleed. People do."

Deep is a short-sword gladiator and veteran who has survived too many wars. He fights on the front line and carries the weight of a man who no longer wants to be used as a symbol.

  • Combat Style: Gladiator / Frontline Melee
  • Signature Weapon: Short sword
  • Squad Synergy: Absorbs frontline pressure, breaks shield formations, and peels enemies off vulnerable teammates.

#Solei — The Swift Runner

"I'm still young. I still doubt. But when I run, I know exactly who I am."

Solei fights with karate-style kicks. Her mechanical legs have demon eyes. She is young, fast, and confident when she moves, even when she still doubts herself.

  • Combat Style: Karate / Kick Combos
  • Signature Feature: Mechanical legs with demon eyes
  • Squad Synergy: Uses fast footwork and kick combinations to pressure enemies and create openings for allies.

#Mark — The Tactical Scout

"Knowing where the enemy will be tomorrow is worth more than a thousand soldiers today."

Mark is a tactical archer with a bow that can split into swords. He wears laser-style glasses, reads the battlefield, and supports the team from range or in close combat. His moveset also includes Grenade, a thrown explosive skill.

  • Combat Style: Ranged Support / Battlefield Disruption
  • Signature Weapon: Bow that can split into swords
  • Signature Feature: Laser-style glasses
  • Squad Synergy: Provides covering arrows, switches to swords when enemies close in, and uses Grenade to disrupt enemy positions.

#Tulas — The Blood Channeler

"Every miracle has a price. Some choose to pay in steel; others, in blood."

Tulas uses blood magic and has a cross mark on his chest. His control of blood and liquids can create barriers, platforms, temporary paths, and offensive tools, but his power always carries a moral cost.

  • Combat Style: Area Denial / Platform Conjurer
  • Signature Feature: Blood magic / Cross mark on his chest
  • Squad Synergy: Creates defensive barriers and vertical paths, controlling the flow of the battlefield and shielding allies from harm.

#Block — The Young Guardian

"Call me boy or call me man. I'll stand on my own two feet and fight with my fists."

Block is a Black boxer who punches, kicks, guards, and counters. Saving him is one of the first campaign objectives, and his fighting style makes him a dependable protector of his teammates.

  • Combat Style: Boxing / Punches, Kicks, and Guards
  • Squad Synergy: Holds his ground with a boxing guard and uses punches and kicks to protect allies.

#Ghost — The Nameless Survivor

"My record is blank. My future is mine to write."

Ghost begins as a nameless survivor pulled out of Jamerson's prison. The system cannot fully identify him, and that failure may be the reason the relic fragments cannot control him like everyone else.

  • Combat Style: Unpredictable Skirmisher / Phase Duelist
  • Signature Weapon: Fragmented Phase Blade
  • Squad Synergy: Bypasses enemy guards completely, creates temporal decoys, and disrupts boss spell-casting.
